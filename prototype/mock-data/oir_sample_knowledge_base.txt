Tunghai University — Office of International Relations
Information Guide for International Students

1. ALIEN RESIDENT CERTIFICATE (ARC) REGISTRATION

All international students staying in Taiwan for more than six months must apply
for an Alien Resident Certificate within 15 days of arrival. Applications are
submitted to the National Immigration Agency office in Taichung City.

Required documents for the application: your passport with a valid visitor or
resident visa, one 2-inch photograph taken within the last six months, the
enrollment certificate issued by the Office of Academic Affairs, and the
application fee of NT$1,000 for a one-year certificate.

Processing normally takes ten working days. You will receive a text message when
the certificate is ready for collection. Late applications may be fined between
NT$2,000 and NT$10,000, so students are strongly advised not to delay.

Renewal must be started thirty days before your current ARC expires. Students who
change their residential address must report the new address to the immigration
office within fifteen days of moving.


2. DORMITORY OPTIONS AND HOUSING APPLICATION

Tunghai University offers three dormitory types for international students.

Shared four-person rooms cost approximately NT$12,000 per semester and include
air conditioning, a shared bathroom on each floor, and laundry facilities on the
ground floor.

Two-person rooms cost approximately NT$18,000 per semester and include a private
bathroom within the room. A limited number of single rooms are available at
NT$28,000 per semester, allocated by lottery with priority given to graduate
students.

Applications open two months before each semester begins. International students
receive priority allocation during their first year of study. Dormitory fees are
paid together with tuition and are non-refundable after the second week of class.

Curfew does not apply to the international student dormitories, but overnight
guests must be registered at the front desk before 10 PM.


3. COURSE CREDIT TRANSFER POLICY

Exchange and degree-seeking students may apply to transfer credits earned at
their home institution. Applications must be submitted to the Office of Academic
Affairs within the first four weeks of the semester.

A maximum of one third of the total credits required for the degree may be
transferred. Each course submitted for transfer must have a syllabus in English
or Chinese, an official transcript from the issuing institution, and a grade
equivalent to C or better.

The relevant academic department reviews each request and decides whether the
course content sufficiently matches an equivalent Tunghai course. Decisions are
usually issued within one month of submission. Rejected applications may be
appealed once, within two weeks of notification.


4. SCHOLARSHIPS AND FINANCIAL SUPPORT

The Tunghai International Student Scholarship covers full or partial tuition for
students with outstanding academic records. Applications are reviewed twice per
year, in May and November.

The Taiwan Ministry of Education Scholarship provides a monthly stipend of
NT$15,000 plus a tuition waiver for up to two years for master's degree students.
Applicants must hold a valid resident visa and maintain a grade average above 80.

Work-study positions in the university library and administrative offices pay
NT$183 per hour for a maximum of twenty hours per week. International students
must obtain a work permit from the Ministry of Labor before starting any paid
work in Taiwan. Working without a permit may result in visa cancellation.


5. LANGUAGE REQUIREMENTS

Degree programs taught in Chinese require a TOCFL Level 3 certificate or
equivalent proficiency. Programs taught in English require a TOEFL iBT score of
71, an IELTS score of 5.5, or proof of previous education conducted in English.

The university offers free Chinese language classes to all international students
during the first year. Classes meet twice per week in the evening and are divided
into beginner, intermediate, and advanced levels based on a placement test held
during orientation week.

Students who do not meet the language requirement at admission may be admitted
conditionally and must pass the required level before beginning their second year.


6. CAMPUS LIFE GUIDE (校園生活指南)

東海大學位於台中市西屯區，校園面積廣大，設有圖書館、體育館、游泳池與多間餐廳。
圖書館平日開放時間為早上八點至晚上十點，週末開放至下午五點。學生可憑學生證借閱
書籍，一次最多十本，借期為三十天。

校內設有健康中心，提供免費的基本醫療諮詢與急救服務！國際學生必須加入全民健康保險，
保費每月新台幣八百二十六元，由學校代為辦理。健保卡通常在申請後六週內寄達。

學生餐廳提供中式、西式與素食餐點，平均一餐約新台幣八十元。校園內有便利商店與郵局，
營業時間為早上七點至晚上十一點。

社團活動於每學期初開放報名；國際學生社團每月舉辦文化交流活動，歡迎所有學生參加。
校車服務連接校園與台中市區，平日每三十分鐘一班，車資新台幣二十元。


7. HEALTH INSURANCE AND MEDICAL SERVICES

International students who hold a valid ARC and have stayed in Taiwan for more
than six months must enroll in the National Health Insurance program. The monthly
premium is NT$826 and is collected by the university together with tuition fees.

Before becoming eligible for National Health Insurance, students must purchase
private medical insurance covering at least the first six months of their stay.
Proof of coverage is required during registration.

The campus health center provides free basic consultations, first aid, and
referrals to partner hospitals. It is open Monday through Friday from 8 AM to
5 PM. The nearest full-service hospital is approximately fifteen minutes from
campus by bus.


8. ORIENTATION AND ARRIVAL

Orientation week is held one week before classes begin each semester. Attendance
is mandatory for all newly admitted international students and covers course
registration, campus tours, the Chinese placement test, and immigration procedures.

Airport pickup service from Taichung International Airport and Taoyuan
International Airport is available if requested at least seven days in advance
through the Office of International Relations. The service is free for students
arriving during the designated arrival period.

Students should bring the following on arrival: passport, admission letter,
original academic transcripts, six passport-sized photographs, and proof of
medical insurance. Original degree certificates must be authenticated by a
Taiwanese representative office in the student's home country.
