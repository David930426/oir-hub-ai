2027 Dormitory Fee Adjustments and Rule Changes

Please be advised of the upcoming changes to international student housing starting next semester.

Due to recent renovations, the fee for two-person rooms with private bathrooms has been adjusted from NT$18,000 to NT$20,000 per semester. The fees for shared four-person rooms (NT$12,000) and single rooms (NT$28,000) remain unchanged. 

Furthermore, the overnight guest policy has been updated. While there is still no curfew for residents, overnight guests are no longer permitted during mid-term and final exam weeks to ensure a quiet study environment. At all other times, guests must still be registered at the front desk before 10 PM.
