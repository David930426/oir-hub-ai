New Campus Safety Policy: Electric Scooters

To prevent fire hazards and ensure pedestrian safety, the charging of personal electric scooters, e-bikes, and hoverboards is strictly prohibited inside all Tunghai University academic buildings and dormitories. 

Students must use the designated outdoor e-scooter charging stations located behind the main library and next to the student cafeteria. Additionally, the speed limit for e-scooters on campus roads is now capped at 15 km/h. Violators of the charging or speed policies will face disciplinary action and potential confiscation of the vehicle.
