Tunghai University — Student Life & Housing Guide

1. DORMITORY OPTIONS AND HOUSING APPLICATION
Tunghai University offers three dormitory types for international students. Shared four-person rooms cost approximately NT$12,000 per semester. Two-person rooms cost approximately NT$18,000 per semester. Applications open two months before each semester begins.

2. CAMPUS LIFE AND FACILITIES
The library is open from 8 AM to 10 PM on weekdays. Students can borrow up to 10 books for 30 days. Student restaurants offer meals for an average of NT$80. The campus shuttle bus runs every 30 minutes and costs NT$20.

3. HEALTH INSURANCE
International students who hold a valid ARC and have stayed in Taiwan for more than six months must enroll in the National Health Insurance program. The monthly premium is NT$826.
